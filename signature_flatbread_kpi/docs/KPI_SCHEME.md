# Signature Flatbreads — Maintenance Team KPI & Monthly Bonus Scheme (V2)

## Objective

Improve factory performance by focusing on prevention, not just repair:

- Improve **OEE**
- Reduce **breakdowns** and **downtime**
- Increase machine **reliability**
- Complete **planned maintenance (PM)** on time
- Improve repair effectiveness (**MTTR** / **MTBF**)
- Maintain a **safe** workplace

Scheme principles: Transparent · Fair · Team-based · Measurable · Capped at 100%

## Definitions & formulas

### 1. OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) — 20%

\[
\text{OEE} = \text{Availability} \times \text{Performance} \times \text{Quality}
\]

- Availability = Actual operating time ÷ Planned production time  
- Performance = Actual production rate ÷ Ideal production rate  
- Quality = Good units ÷ Total units  

**Bonus score:** `(Actual ÷ Target) × 100%` (capped at 100%)

### 2. Breakdown Frequency — 10%

Count of unplanned breakdowns per line per month.

**Bonus score:** `(Target ÷ Actual) × 100%` (capped at 100%)  
If Actual = 0, score = 100%.

### 3. Breakdown Hours — 15%

Total hours of downtime caused by breakdowns per line per month.

**Bonus score:** `(Target ÷ Actual) × 100%` (capped at 100%)  
If Actual = 0, score = 100%.

### 4. PM Compliance — 20%

\[
\text{PM} = \frac{\text{Completed PM on time}}{\text{Planned PM}} \times 100\%
\]

**Bonus score:** `(Actual ÷ Target) × 100%` (capped at 100%)

### 5. MTTR (Mean Time To Repair) — 15%

\[
\text{MTTR} = \frac{\text{Total breakdown downtime}}{\text{Number of breakdowns}}
\]

**Bonus score:** `(Target ÷ Actual) × 100%` (capped at 100%)

### 6. MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) — 10%

\[
\text{MTBF} = \frac{\text{Total operational time}}{\text{Number of failures}}
\]

**Bonus score:** `(Actual ÷ Target) × 100%` (capped at 100%)

### 7. Safety — 10%

- Zero LTI → score = 100%  
- Any LTI → this KPI scores 0%, **and** overall bonus is reduced by **50%**  
- Major safety violation → **total bonus = 0%**

---

## Important rules

1. All KPIs are reported **monthly**.
2. Results are **visible to everyone** (transparency).
3. No single KPI score above **100%**; overall score also capped at **100%**.
4. Bonuses are paid on **team** performance.
5. Disqualifiers (bonus stops at 0%):
   - Major safety violation
   - Major EPW
   - Misleading or dishonest reporting
6. Audits:
   - **Green audit** → reward on top of usual bonus
   - **Red audit** → bonus reduced to **50%**

## Closing goal

> Our goal is not to become better at fixing breakdowns. Our goal is to prevent breakdowns from happening. By improving reliability, completing PMs, reducing downtime, supporting OEE and working safely, we will improve factory performance and maximize our monthly bonus.
